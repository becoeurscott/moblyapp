Mobly is the app to find and rent a space in Cameroon. Room, studio, apartment, villa, office, shop, coworking or commercial space: browse listings published by verified owners in Douala, Yaoundé and across the country.

FIND THE RIGHT SPACE
• Long stay or short stay, your choice
• Filter by type of space, city, neighbourhood and budget
• Interactive map to explore listings around you
• Photos, amenities, prices in FCFA and reviews on every listing

CHAT WITH PEACE OF MIND
• Built in messaging with the owner, including photos and voice notes
• In app calls with your phone number hidden
• Your phone number is never shown to other users

PLAN YOUR VISITS
• Request a visit on the date that suits you
• Track your requests and get reminders before each visit

NEVER MISS A GOOD DEAL
• Save your favourite listings
• Save your searches and get notified about new listings

ARE YOU AN OWNER?
• Publish your listing in minutes, with a 7 day free trial
• Receive and manage visit requests
• Track your listing stats: impressions, clicks, contacts

A TRUSTED COMMUNITY
Owners can verify their identity, every listing can be reported and our team moderates content. A tip: never pay anything before visiting a property.

Mobly connects renters and owners. Mobly is not a real estate agency and does not collect any rent.
